    About the project

    A debate is more than a comment thread.

    Argupedia was developed for the Advanced Computing MSc at King's College London, under the supervision of Dr Sanjay Modgil. It explores whether structured interaction can make online debate more legible and constructive.

    Arguments follow established argumentation schemes and critical questions. Their attack relationships form a graph based on Dung's abstract argumentation frameworks. Grounded, complete and preferred semantics reveal that a debate may have one defensible position, several alternatives, or no settled position at all.

    This restored edition runs entirely in the browser. Debates stay on your device unless you download their JSON file and share it yourself.
